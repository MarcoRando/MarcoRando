## About

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher with a joint affiliation with Inria Sophia Antipolis, in the McTAO team, and Université Côte d’Azur, in the Laboratoire J.A. Dieudonné (LJAD). I am supervised by Samuel Vaiter and Jean-Baptiste Caillau.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Genoa, where I was supervised by Lorenzo Rosasco and Silvia Villa.

My research focuses on **black-box and zeroth-order optimization**, namely the design, implementation, and theoretical analysis of optimization algorithms in settings where first-order information (gradients) is not available. I study these methods across a broad range of settings, including **deterministic and stochastic environments, smooth and non-smooth objectives, convex and non-convex problems, compositional functions, and bilevel optimization**. My interests include **finite-difference methods, structured sampling, variance reduction, zeroth-order bilevel optimization, and Langevin-based methods** for non-convex optimization.

A related direction of my research concerns **optimization over probability distributions**, with applications to **large language models (LLMs)**. I am particularly interested in **memory-efficient zeroth-order methods for LLM pre-training and fine-tuning**, as well as **fast first-order methods for reinforcement-learning-based optimization**, where efficiently estimating the REINFORCE gradient while reducing the cost of sampling is a central challenge. These methods are relevant to both **RLHF-based fine-tuning and adversarial attacks** on language models.

I am also interested in applying **machine learning and optimization to scientific and engineering problems**, including **high-energy physics, robotics, biology, and large-scale machine learning**. My work includes **reinforcement learning for olfactory navigation in turbulent environments**, **gradient-free optimization for tuning humanoid robot walking controllers**, **scalable kernel methods for large-scale machine learning**, and **optimization-based techniques for adversarial machine learning**, with applications including malware detection and large language models.
