## About

I am a researcher working on optimization and machine learning,
with particular interests in zeroth-order optimization,
Langevin methods, and large language models.

## Research Areas

### Zeroth-order Optimization

Development, theoretical analysis, and computational implementation
of optimization methods for settings where only function evaluations
are available.

### Langevin Methods for Non-convex Optimization

Development, theoretical analysis, and computational implementation
of Langevin-based optimization methods for non-convex problems.
